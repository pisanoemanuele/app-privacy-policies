# Privacy Policy — iVat CalcPro

Last updated: June 2026

iVat CalcPro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to external servers.

All data — including your calculation history and app settings — is stored exclusively on your device. No one, including the developer, can access your data.

The Conversion mode fetches live currency exchange rates from a third-party service (exchangerate-api.com) through an anonymous request containing no personal data; when offline, the app uses the rates last cached locally on your device.

iVat CalcPro does not use analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs of any kind.
